Engineering intelligence. Building businesses.

I’m Maryam, an AI engineer and AI entrepreneur. I work where artificial intelligence, software engineering and business development meet, because technology only matters when it solves a problem someone needs solved.

I start with the business challenge, not the model. Factories, supply chains and enterprises make costly decisions with incomplete information, and I build systems that put the right insight in front of the right person at the right time.

That approach has taken me into textile manufacturing and cold chain logistics, and it keeps pushing me toward new ventures that turn complex operations into intelligent, usable products.
